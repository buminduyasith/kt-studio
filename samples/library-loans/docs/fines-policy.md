# Loans and Fines Policy

*Maintained by Circulation Services. Last reviewed by M. Lindqvist, Head of Circulation, August 2026.*

This page is the policy staff and members should rely on. If the desk system
behaves differently, tell Circulation so we can raise it with the developers.

## How long can I keep an item?

- Most items are lent for **21 days**.
- Items on the **New Releases** shelf are lent for **7 days**, so more people get a turn.
- A member can have **up to 5 items** on loan at once (overdue items count towards this).

## Renewals

- You can renew a loan **twice**. Each renewal gives you a fresh loan period starting from the day you renew.
- You cannot renew if someone else has **reserved** the item. Please return it so they can collect it.
- You cannot renew an item once it is **late**. Return it and borrow it again if nobody is waiting.

## Overdue items and fines

- An item becomes **overdue** the day after its due date. We send a reminder email
  on the 1st, 3rd, 7th, 14th and 30th day an item is late.
- We allow a **2-day grace period**. No fine is charged for the first two days late.
- From the 3rd day late, the fine is **$0.25 per day**.
- Fines are **capped at $20.00 per item**, no matter how late it is.
- Fines are added to your account when you return the item.

## Paying fines and borrowing privileges

- You can pay fines at any desk or online, in full or in part. We cannot accept more than you owe.
- If you owe **more than $10.00**, you cannot borrow until you pay your balance down to $10.00 or less.
  Renewing and returning are still allowed.

## Examples

| Days late | Fine   |
|-----------|--------|
| 1-2       | $0.00  |
| 3         | $0.25  |
| 10        | $2.00  |
| 30        | $7.00  |
