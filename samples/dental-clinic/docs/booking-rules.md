# Booking rules (front desk)

Written by the clinic manager. If the app disagrees with this page, tell the developers.

- We are open Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 5 pm. Lunch is 12 to 1; nobody is booked over lunch.
- Appointments start every 15 minutes.
- A check-up takes 30 minutes, a cleaning 45, a filling an hour, a root canal an hour and a half.
- Book at least 2 hours ahead, and at most 60 days ahead.
- A patient can have at most 3 upcoming visits.
- Cancelling less than 24 hours before the visit costs a late fee of Rs. 25.00.
- Patients check in at the desk from 30 minutes before their visit until 15 minutes after.
- The patient gets a text message when we book, and a reminder the day before.
